Arbitrage runs on volume: dozens of fresh videos every week. And it always breaks on editors — expensive, slow and unwilling to work. Arbicut replaces them: a team of AI agents that makes traffic videos on its own.

Who it’s for

Arbitrage specialists and teams who drive traffic from TikTok, Reels and Shorts into Telegram channels. No editing skills needed.

The problem

Editors cost money, miss deadlines and disappear. One person makes a couple of videos a day at best — and the whole funnel depends on their mood.

How it helps

A reel in 15 minutes instead of a day. As many as you need, every day, for the price of a subscription. Built on a formula that steadily gets 10–12K views.

01TopicYou type one line: what the video is about.
02ScriptThe writer agent drafts 10 hooks and a 30-second script.
03VisualsThe designer agent builds the scenes and characters.
04EditVoice-over, word-by-word captions, transitions, music, sound.
05ReadyThe agent watches the reel and fixes it. You get final.mp4, a caption and hashtags.
